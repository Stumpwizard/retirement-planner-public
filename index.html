Important limitations

Brokerage capital gains taxes and HSA nonqualified distributions are not modeled; brokerage withdrawals are provisionally treated as cash-flow proceeds, and HSA distributions are limited to the entered qualified medical budget. Do not use these results for tax decisions. This is an illustrative deterministic planning tool, not a Monte Carlo probability-of-success calculation. Federal taxes are approximated using a 2026 single-filer bracket baseline inflated by 2.5% per year; Indiana state tax is modeled as a configurable flat rate, with no county tax. Social Security taxation is estimated with federal provisional-income rules. IRA withdrawals are assumed taxable; the Roth is used after traditional IRA depletion and assumed qualified. Unfunded spending shortfalls are shown after depletion. Taxes after depletion and during partial Roth-funded years are only approximated. RMDs are approximated using the Uniform Lifetime Table starting at age 75; excess distributions, Medicare premiums/IRMAA, ACA subsidy effects, other income, tax credits, actual fund dividends and changing laws are not fully modeled. The first retirement year excludes wages earned before retirement. Tax figures are estimates, not tax advice. Review with a qualified financial/tax adviser before acting.
